# 이다

이다 is neither a verb nor an adjective but can be conjugated.

Unlike in English, 이다 is *not* used to indicate that something “is” an adjective. 이다 is used to indicate that a noun *is* a noun.

### Special Rules:

- Like adjectives, 이다 can not act on an object. Only verbs can act on objects. It would be incorrect to include the object particle 을/를 on the second noun.
- 이다 is attached directly to the last noun. Verbs and adjectives are not attached to nouns like this, but 이다 is. Including a space between the second noun and 이다 would be incorrect.
