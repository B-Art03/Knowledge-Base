# 에

에 indicates location and time. It is possible to use **~에** more than once in a sentence.

## Location

When a position word is used the ~에 is attached to it rather than the noun. 
Example: 학교 **앞**에 = in front of the school

에 can also be used to denote a place someone goes to

## Time

에 can also be attached to the days of the week.
