# Lesson 8

## Marking Place and Time with ~에

The ~에 particle marks time and location. Examples:

저는 병원**에** 갔어요 - I went to the/a hospital
저는 저녁**에** 공부했어요 - I studied in the evening

---

If you have a location word it will be attached to the location word. Example:

저는 병원을 공원 옆**에** 지었어요 = I built a hospital beside the park

---

When it comes to time you do **not** add them to the word 오늘, 내일 and 어제. Example:

저는 한국에 오늘 도착했어요 - I arrived in Korea today

## Adverbs

Many Korean adverbs are formed by adding a ~게 to the stem. Examples:

쉽다 -> 쉽게
다르다 -> 다르게

---

Some are irregular or follow a different pattern. Examples:

많다 -> 많이
빠르다 -> 빨리

---

For some 하다 verbs both forms are correct. Examples:

조용하다 -> 조용하게/조용히
안전하다 -> 안전하게/안전히

Often one variant will be more usual.

---

Some adverbs are not formed from nouns as above, which includes:

자주 - often
바로 - immediately / directly

---

Adverbs are placed before the verb. Example:

저는 집에 바로 갔어요 - I went home immediately

## Negative Sentences

### Negative Words

Some words are just the negative of another, such as 싫다 and 싫어하다 (for 좋다 and 좋아하다 but only as in "dislike" not "being bad"), 없다 (for 있다) or 아니다 (for 이다).

### Negation with 안

You can also negate verbs by placing 안 before them such as:

안 먹었어요 - I didn't eat

### Negation with ~지 않다

The same as with 안 can be achieved with ~지 않다. For example:

먹지 않았어요 - I didn't eat

Notice that the tense is also on 않다 now.

This variant tends to sounds more formal.
